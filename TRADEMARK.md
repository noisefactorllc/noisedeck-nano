**We do not claim any rights to the word "Nano."** The term is generic and widely used. This Policy applies narrowly to prevent consumer confusion in contexts directly connected to this software project.

You **may** use "Noisedeck Nano," "Noisedeck," and "Noise Factor" without prior permission in the following circumstances:

- **Attribution:** Referring to the project by name in documentation, articles, tutorials, or other descriptive contexts (e.g., "This firmware is based on Noisedeck Nano" or "Built with Noisedeck Nano").

- **Community Discussion:** Discussing the project in forums, social media, or community channels.

When creating derivative works or products **based on or incorporating this software**, you **may not**:

- **Use "Noisedeck" or "Noise Factor"** as the name, or part of the name, of your product, service, or derivative work.
- **Imply Official Status:** Present your derivative as the official Noisedeck Nano project, or as endorsed by or affiliated with Noise Factor.
- **Create Confusion:** Use names, logos, splash screens, or branding designed to create confusion between your derivative and this project. The boot splash and the "by noisefactor.io" credit in this firmware are part of that branding; replace them in a derivative.

## 5. Derivative Works

If you create a derivative work based on this software:
